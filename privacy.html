Privacy Policy: Vendor Bill Loader

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Vendor Bill Loader is a private, internal tool used only by Cristaldo Associates, Inc ("we"). It is not offered to the public or to any third party.

What the tool does

It reads vendor invoice and payment information from our own Excel files and creates the corresponding Bills and Bill Payments in our own QuickBooks Online company, using the QuickBooks Online Accounting API.

Data we access

Only our own company's QuickBooks vendor, account, bill and payment records, and our own spreadsheets.

Storage and security

Access tokens and credentials are stored only on a company-controlled computer, in protected storage. Data is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to third parties, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and disconnection

We may keep local logs and review files containing vendor names and amounts for bookkeeping. The connection can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings, then Apps) or by the QuickBooks company administrator.
